A shortfall period has less operating cash available than debt service due. Earlier cash can cover a later shortfall only if the distribution policy retains it.

The minimum retention cap is the smallest end-of-period cash limit that covers every modeled payment from a zero opening balance. It uses the entire scenario path. Funding needed measures the gap at a payment date.
